# Assessment source notes

Last reviewed: September 9, 2026.

The question bank uses job-related restaurant scenarios and is written to avoid clinical or diagnostic claims. Managers should confirm that each scored topic remains connected to the actual duties of the position and should periodically review selection rates and outcomes.

## Alabama alcohol service and youth employment

- Alabama ABC Board, [Responsible Vendor Program](https://alabcboard.gov/licensing-compliance/responsible-vendor-program)
- Alabama Legislature, [Code of Alabama](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama)

Alabama ABC states that people under 21 generally may not dispense or serve alcohol. It also describes a limited exception for 18–20-year-old servers at specified restaurant, hotel-restaurant, or special-retail licensees that are certified in the Responsible Vendor Program. The operating license, RVP status, employee training, assigned task, and current law must be verified before assigning alcohol duties.

## Food safety and sushi controls

- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, [2022 Food Code](https://www.fda.gov/food/fda-food-code/food-code-2022)
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, [Supplement to the 2022 Food Code](https://www.fda.gov/media/183271/download)
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, [Fish and Fishery Products Hazards and Controls Guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/80637/download)

FDA Food Code provisions are a model; the restaurant must follow the requirements adopted by its actual regulatory authority plus its approved HACCP plan, variance, license conditions, supplier documentation, and written house procedures. For that reason, questions involving sushi rice acidification, time as a public-health control, parasite destruction, and specialized processing direct candidates to the operation's approved plan rather than presenting one recipe as universally controlling.

## Beverage recipes

- International Bartenders Association, [Official Cocktails](https://iba-world.com/cocktails/)

Common-recipe questions measure recognition of widely used classics. During service, the current approved house recipe and measured-pour policy control.

## Limitations

These sources support the operational topics used in the test. They do not by themselves establish local legal compliance, content validity, criterion validity, or absence of adverse impact. A qualified Alabama employment attorney and the applicable health/alcohol authorities should review the final production program and any later changes.
